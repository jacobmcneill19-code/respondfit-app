The short version

  • We collect what we need to run your training plan, including some health and fitness information.
  • Your wellbeing check-ins and your date of birth stay on your phone. Only the age worked out from your date of birth is saved to your account. Your other training data is saved to your account so it is backed up and works across devices.
  • We never sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • You can see, correct, download or delete your information at any time. Email app@respondfit.com.

1. Who we are

RespondFit is a fitness app for firefighters, police, paramedics, rescue and SES crews, defence personnel and people preparing to join a service. It is run by Respond Fit.app Australia ("we", "us"). This policy explains how we handle your personal information under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles. It covers the RespondFit app at respondfitapp.com and our website at respondfit.com.

2. What we collect

Account details: your name, email address and password. Your password is handled by our sign-in provider; we never see it.

Health and fitness information that you choose to enter, such as:

  • your age (worked out from your date of birth, see below), height, weight, body measurements and progress photos
  • workouts, sets, weights, running and beep test results, and fitness test targets and results
  • injuries or limitations you tell us about, daily habits such as water, sleep, steps and protein, and meal plans and food diary entries
  • your service, roster pattern and training goals
  • wellbeing check-ins on mood, stress, energy and shifts. These are stored only on your device.
  • your date of birth, if you enter it in your profile. This is stored only on your device. It is not sent to us or saved in the cloud, and your coach cannot see it. The app uses it to work out your age and to show you a birthday message. Only the age is saved to your account.

Things you share with others: Community posts, photos, comments and likes, and Crew Challenge entries.

Membership details: whether your membership is active. Payments are processed by Stripe; we do not receive or store your full card details.

Technical information needed to run the app, such as sign-in records and basic device and browser information.

Health information is sensitive information under Australian privacy law. We only collect it with your consent, which you give when you create your account, and only to provide the app's features.

3. How we use it

  • to create and secure your account and check your membership
  • to build your training, test-prep and meal plans, track your progress and calculate your scores
  • to back up your data and restore it when you sign in on a new device
  • to run Community and Crew Challenge features
  • to let a coach you are connected with support your training
  • to answer your questions and feedback, and to tell you about important changes to RespondFit
  • to keep the app safe and working, and to meet our legal obligations

We will only send you marketing emails if you have agreed to receive them, and you can unsubscribe at any time. We do not sell your personal information or use your health information for advertising.

4. Who can see your information

  • You. Everything you enter is visible to you.
  • Your coach. If you are connected with a RespondFit coach, they can see your training data, your age and the progress photos you add. They cannot see your date of birth.
  • Other members. Only what you post in Community or Crew Challenge, along with your first name.
  • Service providers who help us run RespondFit: Google Firebase (sign-in, database and file storage), Stripe (payments), GitHub (hosting the app and website), and Open Food Facts (when you scan a food barcode, only the barcode number is sent). They may only use your information to provide their service to us.
  • Others where the law requires it, or where we reasonably believe it is needed to prevent a serious threat to someone's life, health or safety.

5. Where it is stored and how we protect it

Your account data is stored with Google Firebase. Some of our providers, including Google, Stripe and GitHub, may store or process information outside Australia, including in the United States. We choose providers with strong security and take reasonable steps to make sure they handle your information in line with Australian privacy law.

Data is encrypted when it is sent and stored by these providers, and security rules limit each member to their own data. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong password that you do not use anywhere else. If a data breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we will tell you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner, as the law requires.

6. Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your information while your account is open. If you ask us to close your account, we will delete your personal information within 30 days, except where we must keep some records by law (for example, payment records). You can delete the data stored on your device at any time from Profile → Reset.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Ask for a copy of your information. You can also download a backup from Profile → Your data.
  • Ask us to correct anything that is wrong.
  • Withdraw your consent or close your account. If you do, we will not be able to provide the app's features.

To do any of these, email app@respondfit.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Not medical advice

RespondFit gives general fitness, nutrition and wellbeing guidance. It is not a medical service and does not replace advice from a doctor or health professional.

9. Complaints

If you have a privacy concern, email app@respondfit.com and we will respond within 30 days. If you are not happy with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as RespondFit changes. We will show the new version in the app and, if the changes are significant, ask for your agreement again. The version number and date at the top show which version is current.
